## What the app does

Claim Check compares an insurance claim recorded in Jira with a policy wording kept in Confluence and shows an indicative assessment with the clauses it relied on. The assessment is a reading aid. It is not insurance, legal or financial advice, and it does not decide whether a claim is covered. The coverage decision always remains with your claims handlers.
